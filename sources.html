Effective-date provenance

DeepSeek publishes no effective timestamp for the current table. Flash prices were reduced on 2026-09-10 (API changelog, date only), and the holiday exclusion appeared on the pricing pages between observations on 2026-09-12 and 2026-10-04, with no change date published. The calculator therefore offers estimates only from 2026-10-05 22:40:57 UTC, the capture that verified the current table, onward. Earlier times are unavailable rather than priced on a guess.

Coverage gaps

  • No announced future rate basis appeared on either live page at build time.
  • DeepSeek does not list which dates count as Chinese public holidays. The Chinese page says 法定节假日 (statutory public holidays); whether make-up rest days within a holiday break count is not stated. The calculator asks you instead of guessing.
  • No provider-side per-request invoice or billing ledger is included. The tool checks declared prices, not actual charges.
  • The authority pages can change after this static build. The as-of timestamp is part of every estimate's evidence.
  • The current pages reserve the right to adjust prices. This project makes no maintenance or freshness promise.

Source gaps are displayed, never converted into a clean bill of health.
