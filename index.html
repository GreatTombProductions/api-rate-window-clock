The rule the calculator applies

Peak is a weekday condition, not just an hour.

Peak rates apply Monday–Friday at 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC, except on Chinese public holidays. Every other hour is off-peak, including those same hours on Saturday, Sunday and Chinese public holidays. Window starts are included; window ends are not.

What this estimate does—and does not—prove

It uses the declared table.

English and Chinese vendor pages were parsed independently. A structural disagreement disables the estimate rather than producing an average.

It preserves token classes.

Cache-hit input, cache-miss input, and output are multiplied by their own rates. A blended scalar would hide the batch's actual economics.

It is not an invoice audit.

The result excludes taxes, retries, credits, reseller terms, and provider-side token-count differences. It estimates one start-time band and does not model a job crossing windows.

Static, open source, and private by construction. No API key or usage data is requested.
